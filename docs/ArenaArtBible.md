# DEADLAST - Arena Art Bible

## Visual Target

DeadLast is a cartoon neon arena game.

The player should feel seated at a dramatic game-show table, not looking at a card UI.

Reference style:
- Cartoon 3D
- Neon-lit arena
- Futuristic casino/game-show energy
- Colorful but dark enough for dramatic reveals
- Big readable shapes
- Fun, not gritty

## Camera Rule

The camera never rotates.

The local player is always front and center at the bottom of the screen.

## Seat Layouts

## Arena Elements

Required:
- Large circular table
- Neon edge lighting
- Player stations
- Pedestals
- Avatar seats
- Dramatic background crowd/stadium
- Center DEADLAST emblem or symbol

## UI Rule

The arena is the UI.

Avoid:
- Floating card grids
- Big flat menus
- Scrolling gameplay areas
- Generic web-app panels

## Color Direction

Primary:
- Black / deep navy background
- Electric cyan
- Hot magenta
- Neon green
- Gold highlights
- Red for Dead Last danger moments

## Signature Moment

Countdown ends.

Arena goes silent.

Lights dim.

All hands reveal at the same time.

Impact flash.

Crowd reacts.

This should become DeadLast's iconic moment.
