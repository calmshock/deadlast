- Nintendo

---

# Idle

Characters should never stand perfectly still.

Every avatar has:

- breathing
- blinking
- subtle sway

Arena lights softly pulse.

Crowd gently moves.

---

# Countdown

3

Arena bright.

2

Lights pulse.

1

Music drops.

Crowd quiets.

GO

Impact flash.

---

# Choosing

Current player glows.

Pedestal pulses.

Hand hovers.

Timer slowly shrinks.

---

# Lock In

Player leans forward.

Hand slams onto pedestal.

Small shockwave.

Pedestal lights up.

Character returns to idle.

---

# Reveal

All players reveal simultaneously.

Camera pushes slightly toward the table.

Arena lights brighten.

Reveal lasts approximately 500–700 ms.

---

# Winner

Chair rises slightly.

Gold spotlight.

Confetti.

Crowd cheers.

Avatar celebrates.

Coins sparkle briefly.

---

# Dead Last

Red spotlight.

Chair lowers slightly.

Smoke puff.

Crowd boos.

Avatar slumps.

"DEAD LAST" flashes dramatically.

Never cruel—always playful.

---

# Camera

The camera never spins.

Movement is subtle.

Allowed:

- Push in
- Pull back
- Tiny shake
- Slight bounce

Never rotate around the arena.

---

# Timing Guidelines

Idle:
Continuous

Countdown:
3 seconds

Reveal:
0.6 seconds

Reaction:
1 second

Celebration:
2 seconds

Round reset:
Under 1 second

Entire round target:
15–20 seconds

---

# UI Animation

Panels slide.

Numbers count upward.

Buttons bounce slightly.

Hover effects are playful.

No abrupt changes.
