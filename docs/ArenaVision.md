# DEADLAST - Arena Vision

## Core Philosophy

DeadLast is NOT a Rock Paper Scissors game.

DeadLast is a competitive party arena where Rock Paper Scissors is the mechanic.

Players should immediately recognize DeadLast from a screenshot.

---

# Design Rules

No floating windows.

No card grids.

No scrolling.

The arena IS the UI.

---

# Animation Philosophy

Every action should have:

Anticipation

Movement

Impact

Reaction

Recovery

Nothing should instantly appear.

Everything should animate.
